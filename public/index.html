Features

Everything your bot needs to run

No dashboards to learn, no configs to fight. Upload it, and we take it from there.

One-click deploy

Drop a ZIP or connect a repo. That's genuinely it — no server to configure, no dependencies to chase down by hand.

$ corex deploy ./mybot.zip
✓ uploaded · 1.2 MB
✓ container started in 2.8s
→ your bot is live

Always online

Your bot runs around the clock in its own container. We're the ones losing sleep over uptime, not you.

Live console

Logs stream to your browser as they happen. No SSH keys, no terminal apps to install first.

Secret env vars

Your token stays a secret — injected at runtime, never sitting in your code where someone could find it.

Full isolation

One bot misbehaving never touches another. Every container gets its own lane, CPU, and memory cap.

Resource monitoring

Live CPU and RAM graphs, right on your bot's page — so you see the scale-up moment before it becomes a problem.
